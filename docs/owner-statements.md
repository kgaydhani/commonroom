# The owner's statements

What the owner said about Common Room, in his words, as typed. This is the source that [axioms.md](axioms.md) and [design-v3.md](design-v3.md) answer to. Both are an agent's distillation. **Where they differ from this page, this page wins.**

**How to read it**

- Read this before the design. A session or worker that has only the design has the mechanisms and not the reason for them.
- What he wants and why is the brief. What he says about how is a guess, by his own account, and should be weighed, researched and replaced when something serves the outcome better.
- He has let the room go: decide, act, and report with evidence. Do not hand decisions back to him. Ask only where his answer would change what gets built and research cannot supply it, or where it needs his money or his accounts.
- Add to this page whenever he says something durable, with the date and his words. Never edit or remove a statement. If he changes his mind, add the new statement and note which earlier one it replaces.
- Personal details are left out, because the repository is public. Text in square brackets replaces a name.

## His suggestions about how

These are his guesses at mechanisms. He said how to treat them:

> its way more important to me, that u are trying ot get my outputs and vision and udnerstanding of why im doing all of this and what i want, rather than any of the things i decide for how u should do it = because lets be real, i have no clue, im just guessing and everytime u do actual research, ur way better than i could ever be.

- **Offering to attach a session** (13 Nov): "a very cheap identifier that every session does, which is to see if a prompt is an appropriate common room thing [...] this is when something would kick in that asks the user, would you like to move this session onto common room. then when the user says yes, it would ask the question of whether/how to reorganise the pie ( + a not sure yet option)."
- **A bank** (13 Nov): "having a bank of available skills, agents as well perfected self governance and self management tools and protocols so that the 'administrator' pool, whatevr that means, can efficiently and optimally delegate and report back."
- **An interface** (20 Nov): "there should be a ui [...] a separate settings session that runs as a dumb llm that is whats used to define projects (names titles config etc) as well as usage allocation, and any custom settings that depend user to user. then the main ui should be split into tabs depending on active projects, and u can see how much of their separate (fuzzy) usage meters are used either for the day or the week, and then u can see like what is currently working, recent file updates (prs or merges or whatever) and then an interface that is the mirror of your in session chat that started it [...] and then u can see how ur messages are being synthesised by the army of agents working in the project itself"
